Choose the right cap

Silicone, latex, or lycra?

Silicone is our pick for most swimmers: comfortable, durable, and it protects hair well. Latex is thinner and lighter, but can tear more easily and some people are allergic to it. Lycra is soft and cloth-like, but less snug and less protective in the water.

Fit that feels right

Choose adult or child size, and tell us if you have long or thick hair so we can help you pick a comfortable fit. A good cap should feel snug without pulling, and stay in place when you swim.

Care, in 30 seconds

Rinse in fresh water after swimming, let it air-dry away from direct sun, and keep it away from sharp objects. That keeps the print bright and the cap lasting longer.

How do I order?
