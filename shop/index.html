Choose the right cap

Silicone, latex, or lycra?

Silicone is our pick for most swimmers: comfortable, durable, and it protects hair well. Latex is thinner and lighter, but can tear more easily and some people are allergic to it. Lycra is soft and cloth-like, but less snug and less protective in the water.

Fit that feels right

Choose adult or child size, and tell us if you have long or thick hair so we can help you pick a comfortable fit. A good cap should feel snug without pulling, and stay in place when you swim.

Care, in 30 seconds

Rinse in fresh water after swimming, let it air-dry away from direct sun, and keep it away from sharp objects. That keeps the print bright and the cap lasting longer.

Questions, answered

How long will my order take?

About 3–4 weeks in total. We confirm your total first, then make your cap, then send it to you.

What does shipping cost?

Shipping is charged separately and confirmed before payment, by destination. We will tell you the exact shipping amount with your confirmed total — no surprises.

How do I order?

Choose a design, start your order with the button above, send us your name, colours, and size, and we confirm your total before you pay.

Can I add my own name or colours?

Yes. That is what our custom caps are for. Send us your name and the colours you like, and we will show you a preview before you pay.

What if I am not sure about the fit?

Tell us your size and hair type when you order, and we will help you choose. Our guides above explain silicone, latex, and lycra in plain words.

Can I return a custom cap?

Custom caps are made for you, so please check your name, colours, and design carefully in the preview before you pay. If something is wrong with your cap when it arrives, contact us through your order and we will help.
